Welcome to Gielinor Travels

Gielinor Travels is a unique way of testing your knowledge of the world of Old School RuneScape, Gielinor, and your ability to quickly think of the best routes you can take. Each level presents you with a different location in Gielinor, and your task is to identify it as quickly as possible. The game combines elements of geography, memory, and quick thinking, making it a fun and challenging experience for RuneScape fans and geography enthusiasts alike.

Compete with other players globally as you attempt to travel to various locations across Gielinor in the shortest time possible. The quicker you arrive at your destination, the higher your score, and the better your chances of climbing the global leaderboard.

Whether you're a seasoned RuneScape veteran or a geography enthusiast, Gielinor Travels offers a unique and engaging gaming experience that combines two worlds into one. To download, simply type in "Gielinor Travels" into the RuneLite plugin hub and click "Install". A warning will appear stating that the plugin will submit data to a third-party website, this is just the leaderboard and is necessary for the plugin to work. Once you've installed the plugin, you can start playing by clicking on the Gielinor Travels icon in the RuneLite sidebar and clicking "Join Lobby"!
